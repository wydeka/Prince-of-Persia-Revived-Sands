# Changelog

## v0.1.0-demo
First public demo. Covers the game only up to the moment the army of King Solomon is summoned.

- Soldiers and shield-bearers: faster wind-ups, strikes, turns, strafes and get-ups; shorter idle; shorter stuns after being hit, kicked, rolled into or pushed back.
- Soldier and sergeant damage slightly raised in a few settings. Health is unchanged.
- The Prince recovers faster after hits, stumbles and knockdowns.
- Installer: English by default (Russian available), "Restore everything" button, optional experimental Malik model (off by default).
